## Latest DEX #3 Release: Introducing a New User-Friendly Touchscreen Interface!

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/touchscreen-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/touchscreen-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "touchscreen-coverimage")

Two lesser known core-features of our [DEX 3 DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) are the ability to completely customize the interface using our [free skin designer software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), and that the browser is touch-screen friendly (_Users_ _have “zones” in the library for quick finger scrolling and drag and drop re-ordering_).

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/touchscreenskin-new.jpg?fit=300%2C186&ssl=1 "touchscreenskin-new")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/touchscreenskin-new.jpg?fit=1030%2C638&ssl=1)

Both are on display in this latest _“Touchy”_ skin submission from our friend Emiliano Vargas (_[DJ Vargas](http://www.partydude.se/)_).

This new 16:9 ratio “Touchy” skin is free for all DEX 3 users to download and use with touch-screens, and will re-size automatically to fit other resolutions (_although GUI graphics may warp slightly on some screen resolutions_.) The skin features large transport control buttons for fat fingers, giant rotating virtual DJ platters (_grab-and-scratch_) that display album art and stacked wave-forms with beat-grid which make visual beat mixing easy.
