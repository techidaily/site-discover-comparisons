## Unraveling the Secrets of Harmonic Mixing: Insights From a Pro DJ with Digital DJ Tips

[![Harmonic mixing explained](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/whatisharmonicmixing-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/whatisharmonicmixing-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "What is harmonic mixing cover image")

**Key Detection** for **harmonic mixing** was recently added to DEX 3 DJ software.

[LEARN MORE ABOUT DEX 3 ![Key Detection DJ software](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/keydetection.jpg?fit=222%2C222&ssl=1 "Key Detection For Harmonic Mixing")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

A modified version of the [Camelot Scale Key](http://www.harmonic-mixing.com/Images/camelotHarmonicMixing.jpg) and the [Musical Key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key%5F%28music%29) is automatically analyzed and displayed in both the decks and in the browser (“detect musical key” needs to be enabled in DEX 3’s options, under the general tab) upon loading a track to the deck, or during a batch analyzation of your track library. ([How to scan for Key in DEX 3](https://youtu.be/qBvjiHsrDJo))

Both the **Musical Key** and modified **Camelot Scale** Key are display within the [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) browser, to the far right side within the track tag data blocks. **You can also search by key** in DEX 3 using the method explained in [this tutorial video](https://youtu.be/7Ji2E4Y1Kw4).
